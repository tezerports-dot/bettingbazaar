# Game logic — how rounds and payouts work

The core game runs in **cycles**. There are two cycle lengths:

- **30-minute** cycles, and
- **Full-day** cycles.

Each cycle has two sides you can bet on: **Delhi** and **Bombay**.

## How the winning side is decided

The winning side is the one that received the **smaller total of real user bets**
in that cycle (the **minority** side wins). Because most money is on the majority
side, this is how the house edge works: the crowd's favourite side is the side
that usually loses.

## Payouts

- Winning bets are paid **2×** the bet amount, **minus a 1% platform fee** on the
  winnings.
- Losing bets are not returned.
- Some bets placed by the platform's own managers ("phantom" bets) are used to
  balance pools; they are always treated as losing and never win payouts.

## Placing a bet

- Bets are placed from your wallet balance. Your **deposit balance** (from token
  purchases) can be used to bet; winnings you accumulate go to your withdrawable
  **winnings balance**.
- A small **reserve** portion of your wallet may be allocated when you buy tokens
  and consumed as you bet, as shown in the app.

## Fairness and responsible play

Results depend on how all users bet in a cycle; no one can guarantee a win, and
support cannot change a cycle's outcome. Please play responsibly and only with
money you can afford to lose.
