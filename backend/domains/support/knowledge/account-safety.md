# Account safety, warnings, and balances

## Your two balances

- **Deposit balance** — from buying tokens. **Not withdrawable**; use it to bet.
- **Winnings balance** — from winning bets. **Withdrawable** (after KYC).

Keeping them separate is why deposited money cannot be cashed out directly: you
play with deposits, and you withdraw winnings.

## Warnings and blocks

The platform tracks **warnings** on an account. Warnings are added for actions
like a merchant dispute going against you. If warnings reach the platform limit,
the account is **automatically blocked** pending review.

## Payment-failure flag

If a merchant reports that a payment you claimed was **not received or failed**,
your account is **flagged** for review. To avoid this, only submit a UTR for a
payment you actually completed, for the exact amount, to the exact merchant shown
on the order. A flagged or blocked account can have withdrawals paused until an
admin reviews it.

## If your account is blocked or flagged

Contact human support. An admin can review and, if appropriate, clear the flag or
unblock the account. Support may ask you to verify your identity, but will
**never** ask for your password or OTP.

## Protecting your account

- Enable **two-factor authentication (2FA)** if available in your settings.
- Never share your password, OTP, or KYC documents with anyone.
- Use your own bank/UPI account, matching your KYC name, for deposits and
  withdrawals.
