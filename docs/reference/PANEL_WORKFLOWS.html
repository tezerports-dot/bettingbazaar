1.1 The order state machine

Nine states, and one rule table: ALLOWED_FROM in database/repositories/orders.core.js.

    REJECTED --> CANCELLED : window closed

    PAID --> DISPUTED

    DISPUTED --> COMPLETED : dispute manager
    DISPUTED --> CANCELLED : dispute manager
    PENDING_QUEUE --> CANCELLED
    ASSIGNED --> CANCELLED
    PROCESSING --> CANCELLED
    PAID --> FAILED

The guard lives in the UPDATE's WHERE clause. A caller arriving with a stale idea of the order matches no row and is refused.

Every member move names the member. Accept, decline, confirm, reject-as-unpaid, red flag and the QR scan pass expectMerchant: it is checked under the order lock before the idempotent answer, and again in the WHERE. A member whose order an admin has just handed to a colleague is told "This order has been moved to another member, so nothing was changed. Refresh your orders." (409).

Once the team's tokens are paid out, only COMPLETED may follow. spendForBuy stamps pool_paid_at after asking the state under the order lock; transition() and reassign() then refuse anything else (pool_paid).

REJECTED is new in 2c+. A member's "payment not received" used to CANCEL the buy and hand the team its tokens back on the member's word alone. Now the buy waits in REJECTED with its pool hold intact for rejectedBuyDisputeMinutes (15), written on the database clock in the same transaction, so the sweep, the dispute route and the screen read one instant. The open sell pool that let an order go PENDING_QUEUE → PROCESSING is gone: every order reaches a member through routing.

1.3 How an order reaches a member

Both directions are assigned; nothing is claimed. routingCandidates + assignToTeam (database/repositories/teamRouting.js), on creation, on a decline, and every 30 seconds for whatever is still queued.

  • an APPROVED member of a team that is WORKING (10 approved) or in GRACE (fell below 10 today, IST), whose supervisor holds the order's rail
  • the member ACTIVE, approved, online, not assignment-paused, switched on for this direction
  • under the rail's cap (SystemConfig.teamRouting.concurrency)
  • not barred from this order or this player (order_rejections)
  • USDT: an address on this chain · UPI_BANK buy: holder, account number and IFSC on file
  • a CASH buy: pressed Ready · a CASH sell: no open buy
  • a buy: the team's pool covers it, proved by taking the hold, not by reading a balance

Fewest open orders first, then least recently assigned. The online, Ready, cap and cash-buy questions are asked again under the member's row lock inside the assigning transaction; a candidate who fails them is skipped for the next (§32 S6).

1.4 The pool hold — why a check is not a lock

A buy's tokens are HELD in the team's pool (team_pools.held_paise, the order's pool_held_paise), in the same transaction that assigns it (holdForBuyWithin). Released on every ending and requeue (releaseBuyHold, detachFromTeamWithin), spent once on completion (spendForBuy). One live hold per order: pool_held_paise = 0 is in the WHERE.

This replaced the per-merchant wallet and its escrow, and closed F-018. Eligibility used to be checked on three doors and held on none, so the tokens a buy needed could go to another order before the confirm. The hold IS the assignment now. A sell holds nothing: it adds to the pool when it settles.

4.5 What the player is NOT shown

A player sees an opaque Merchant #<ref>, a deadline, and, only once the member has accepted, where to pay: the member's bank account on a UPI_BANK buy, the scanned QR on a cash buy, the order's chain address on USDT. Never a mobile or a UPI handle, in a view, a timeline message or a display name. Stated honestly: a upi://pay intent carries the payee, so the payer's banking app shows it; a cash machine's QR names the machine, not a person, which is why a QR that pays a mobile is refused.
