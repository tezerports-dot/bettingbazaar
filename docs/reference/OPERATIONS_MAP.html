How to read this

MONEY MOVED a balance or pool changed and a ledger row exists.

WAITING the row is parked; something else must act.

REFUSED nothing moved; the caller was told why, by code.

F-0NN a recorded defect on this path. See the audit register.

Route, job and screen counts come from node scripts/extract-operations-map.mjs; refusal codes, states and predicates were read out of the handler, the service and the SQL each flow names. Where a flow has a known gap it is marked in place rather than described as working. Where something was not verified, it says so.

Topology

One backend, three panels, one database. Everything shared reaches a panel through the API or a realtime stream — never through a file copied between panels. The merchant panel serves two kinds of merchant login: a supervisor, who runs teams and does no orders, and a member, who does the orders.

The order state machine

Nine states, enforced by the order_states_known CHECK. Every transition is a guarded UPDATE: the states it accepts (ALLOWED_FROM in database/repositories/orders.core.js) are in the WHERE clause, so two callers racing produce one winner and one REFUSED, never two. The open sell pool that once let an order leave PENDING_QUEUE straight to PROCESSING is gone: every order reaches a member through routing.

Once the team's tokens are paid out, only COMPLETED may follow

teamPools.spendForBuy stamps order_states.pool_paid_at in the paying transaction, after asking the state under the order lock. From then on transition() and reassign() refuse every move but COMPLETED (pool_paid). Before this (2c+ review), an admin APPROVE on a REJECTED buy paid the player and then answered 409.

Who may dispute from where

A player disputes a PAID order (10 minutes after Paid), a COMPLETED one, or a REJECTED buy inside dispute_window_until. A member red-flags from PROCESSING or PAID, but never their own rejection. The paid-order-timeout sweep disputes a PAID buy the member ignored (disputeRaisedBy='system'). Only a payment dispute the player or the platform raised can suspend whoever loses it.

Player workflows

The player picks a size, waits for a member to accept, and only then sees where to pay. They never see who they are paying beyond Merchant #ref; on a 50,000+ buy they see the member's bank account, which is the one relaxation the owner made (2026-10-03).

Signing up, and being let in

a form, then Telegram verifies

The form creates the account: mobile, password, confirmation, captcha and an invite code (a referral link's code arrives pre-filled and locked). There is no email, no KYC, no Aadhaar and no upload path; Step 1 deleted all of it. Telegram then proves the number and the channel membership. Nothing a bot does grants a session.

Teams, pools and routing

Step 2 replaced per-merchant wallets, scoring, the open pool, the cash-link queue and the platform payment-mode switch with one structure: an admin-approved supervisor on one rail, up to four teams of exactly ten approved members, and one token pool per team.

Fifteen scheduled jobs (backend/startup/cronJobs.js), an optional Redis, and a realtime layer. Several of these are the only thing between a stuck row and a lost balance.

The one to check by hand

order-expiry deliberately does not touch PAID: the player's money is already gone. A PAID buy is moved on by the member, by paid-order-timeout or utr-after-paid-timeout, or by a person; a PAID sell by the hold settlement.

Realtime

48 names in REALTIME_EVENTS.md across three transports

socket.io for browser clients, SSE for the authenticated merchant and admin streams, and the emitters in realtimeEmitters.js. One name per logical change, unique across all three.

  • emitMerchantUpdate('*', …) reaches nobody; use broadcastToMerchants.
  • A panel's SSE client hears only the names in its own list: compare both lists when adding one.
  • order_rejected arrives at the player on socket.io as order_update with event: 'order_rejected' and disputeUntil.
